How to share one baby log with your partner and family

Last checked 2026-11-27

To share a baby log, keep every feed, diaper and nap in one place that all caregivers can open and write to, instead of in separate notebooks or chat threads. A shared notebook by the changing table works at home; a shared app works when caregivers are in different places.

Why separate records cause problems

When each caregiver keeps their own notes, the record of a single day ends up split across phones, paper and messages. The person taking over has to ask when the last feed or diaper was, and the answer depends on who remembers.

Steps to set up a shared log

  1. Agree on one place for the record and stop logging anywhere else.
  2. Decide what everyone logs: at minimum feeds, diapers and sleep.
  3. Log right after it happens, not at the end of the shift.
  4. At each handover, read the latest entries instead of asking.
  5. If something looks unusual, bring the record to your pediatrician rather than guessing from it.

Including grandparents and other caregivers

Grandparents or a babysitter can read and add to the same record. Keep the setup simple for them: a few record types they use often, and one place to look.
