Why Deerly exists

Looking after a young baby is shared work, but the record of it often is not. Typing is hard with a baby in one arm, caregivers text each other to ask when the last bottle was, records end up split across several phones and notebooks, and daycare reports arrive on paper or as images and never make it into the log.

Deerly was built around those problems. The goal is one shared, up-to-date record of a baby's feeds, sleep, diapers and health, so every caregiver knows what happened last without asking.

That is why most of the work went into getting things into the log quickly: voice logging that turns one sentence into several records, widgets that log without opening the app, and an Apple Watch nursing timer. Family logging syncs in about 2 seconds in testing and is listed as free, because a shared log only helps if everyone can use it.

Some choices are about what Deerly keeps. Photos and videos are relayed briefly between family devices and then deleted from the server instead of being stored there permanently. And because many families already have history elsewhere, Deerly imports a full Piyolog export and daycare daily reports.

Deerly also leaves things out. It is a log, not a sleep coaching or parenting advice app; for questions about your baby's health or sleep, ask your pediatrician. The app is in English and Traditional Chinese only, the free plan shows ads, an account is needed to sync and share, and the Apple Watch app is iOS only.

Deerly is made by Kennily (Ken), an independent developer who also makes PriceDog. If something does not work the way you expect, email kennily251107@gmail.com.
