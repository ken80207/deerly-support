How to use Deerly

A short walkthrough of the things most families do in their first days with Deerly: signing in, adding a baby, logging, inviting family and reading the charts.

Get started: sign in and add your baby

  1. Open Deerly and choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. The account is used to back up your records and share them with family.
  2. Answer "What is your baby called?", pick the sex, and tap Start tracking.
  3. To track more than one baby, use Add another baby, or later go to Settings > Add a baby. Each baby is kept separate.
  4. Set the birthday in Settings > Edit baby profile so ages and growth charts line up.

Log a feed, diaper or sleep

  1. On the home screen, pick the record type: a nursing timer, bottle, formula or pumped milk feed, a diaper, sleep, or any of the 30+ other types such as solids, temperature or medicine.
  2. Check the time (for sleep, when the baby fell asleep and woke up) and tap Add.
  3. The home screen shows the last feed and last diaper, so the next caregiver can see what happened without asking.
  4. Forgot to log something? Back-date or edit the entry later; the change syncs to the family. Night mode keeps the screen dark during night feeds.

Log by voice

  1. Tap Voice and allow microphone access the first time.
  2. Say one sentence, for example "120 ml and a dirty diaper". Several things in one sentence are fine.
  3. Tap Send. Deerly turns the sentence into separate records.
  4. To change the recognition language, go to Settings > Voice > Recognition language.
  5. The free plan has a voice usage quota; you can get more by watching an ad or with Pro.

Log from widgets

  1. Add the Deerly widget to your Home Screen from your phone's widget gallery.
  2. Tap bottle, diaper, nursing or sleep/wake on the widget to log without opening the app. You can back-date by 15 minutes up to 2 hours.
  3. On iPhone, add the Lock Screen widget to see the time since the last feed at a glance.
  4. If the widget says no baby is selected, open the app once to pick one.

Apple Watch nursing timer

With the Deerly app on your Apple Watch, you can start, switch sides and pause a breastfeeding timer from your wrist. The Apple Watch app is iOS only.

Invite your partner and family

  1. Go to Settings > Family sharing and tap Create a family.
  2. Tap Invite family to get an invite code and send it. The code is valid for 7 days.
  3. The other person installs Deerly, signs in, and taps Enter an invite code.
  4. From then on, everyone logs the same baby, sees new entries within seconds, and each entry shows who logged it. Family record sharing is free.
  5. If you want, turn on family log notifications to get a notification on this device when someone in the family logs something.

Growth charts and daily stats

  1. Make sure the baby's sex is set in Settings, because WHO reference curves are calculated separately by sex.
  2. Add a height, weight or head size record; the curve appears and shows which percentile it falls in.
  3. Daily milk, sleep and diaper stats are charted automatically from what you log.
  4. For a preterm baby, switch between corrected and actual age.

Import a daycare report or Piyolog history

Deerly can turn a daycare daily report, as an image or PDF, into a day of records, so the paper report does not stay outside the log.

If you used Piyolog before, Deerly can import a full Piyolog export. Import beyond the trial credits requires Pro.

Free plan and Pro

Tracking, all record types, stats, growth charts, family record sharing and recap sharing are always free. The free plan shows ads and includes limited voice logging.

Deerly Pro lets the family see shared photos and videos, raises the voice logging quota, removes the watermark from recap exports, removes ads, continues daycare report and Piyolog import and unlocks a custom baby character. One subscriber covers the family group.

Pro is US$3.99 per month or US$29.99 per year, and the yearly plan includes a 2-month free trial. Family Sharing, No Ads and Voice Pack are each available on their own as a US$0.99 monthly subscription. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled anytime in your App Store account settings.
