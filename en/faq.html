FAQ

Is family sharing free?

Yes. Logging the same baby with your partner or grandparents, with real-time sync, is part of the free plan. Pro is only needed for the family to see shared photos and videos.

How much does Pro cost?

Deerly Pro is US$3.99 per month or US$29.99 per year; the yearly plan includes a 2-month free trial. Family Sharing, No Ads and Voice Pack are also available on their own at US$0.99 per month each.

Do I need an account?

You need to sign in with Apple or Google to sync and share. The account is used to back up your records and share them with family.

How does voice logging work?

Say one sentence, for example "120 ml and a dirty diaper", and Deerly turns it into separate records. The free plan has a usage quota; you can get more by watching ads or with Pro.

Can I move my history from Piyolog?

Yes. Deerly can import a full Piyolog export. Import beyond the trial credits requires Pro.

Can I track more than one baby?

Yes. Each baby is kept separate, and you can back-date or edit entries with sync.

Does Deerly support preterm babies?

Yes. You can switch between corrected and actual age, and growth is shown against WHO growth charts.

Which languages and devices are supported?

The app is in English and Traditional Chinese. On iPhone it requires iOS 17.0 or later, and the Apple Watch nursing timer is iOS only.

Where are my photos and videos stored?

They are relayed briefly between family devices and then deleted from the server. The kept copies stay on your family's phones. On the free plan, only you see your photos and videos.
