Tracking a preterm baby: corrected age vs actual age

Last checked 2026-11-27

Corrected age is your baby's age counted from their due date instead of their birth date. For a baby born early, care teams often refer to corrected age when they talk about growth and milestones. Your pediatrician will tell you which age to use and for how long.

Corrected age and actual age

Actual age counts from the day your baby was born. Corrected age subtracts the time your baby arrived early. Both are useful, and it helps to have both written down in the same place as the rest of the record.

Keeping a useful record

  1. Write down your baby's birth date and due date in the log.
  2. Log height, weight and head circumference when they are measured.
  3. Note which age your care team is using at each appointment.
  4. Bring the record to appointments and ask your pediatrician how to read it.

FAQ

Until when should I use corrected age?

Ask your pediatrician; they will tell you what applies to your baby.
