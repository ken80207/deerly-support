How to log baby feeds one-handed during night feeds

Last checked 2026-11-27

The easiest way to log a night feed one-handed is to remove the typing: use a tap from a widget or a short spoken sentence, and fix details later if needed. If you prefer paper, keep a pen and a simple tally sheet within reach of the chair you feed in.

Why logging at night is hard

At night you are holding the baby, the room is dark and you do not want to unlock a phone and search through menus. Many parents skip the entry and try to remember it in the morning.

Ways to log with one hand

  1. Put the feeding log where your thumb can reach it, such as a Home Screen widget.
  2. Log the start of the feed with one tap, and the amount or side when you finish.
  3. If tapping is still awkward, say the entry out loud and let voice logging write it.
  4. If you forgot, back-date the entry later instead of leaving a gap.
  5. Use a dark screen so checking the log does not wake you up fully.

Keeping the log within reach and back-filling missed entries work with any method.
