PiyoLog alternatives, and how to bring your history along

Last checked 2026-11-27

If you want to move away from PiyoLog, the main question is whether your existing history comes with you. Deerly can import a full Piyolog export; other trackers differ in focus and language support.

Before you switch

  1. Export your history from PiyoLog.
  2. Install the new app and create the baby profile.
  3. Import the export and check a few days against PiyoLog.
  4. Invite the rest of the family once the history looks right.

Where PiyoLog fits

PiyoLog suits parents who prefer its Japanese-style interface and its babysitter companion app PiyoSup.

Checking imported days against the original works for any switch. In Deerly, the Piyolog import is included in the trial credits and continued import requires Deerly Pro.
