Baby Tracker - Newborn Log alternatives for shared logging

Last checked 2026-11-27

Baby Tracker - Newborn Log is a long-established tracker. If you want something different, such as logging by voice, logging from widgets or sharing one log with the whole family, other apps lean those ways. Which one fits depends on how you log and who else cares for the baby.

What to compare

Look at how quickly you can log with a baby in your arms, whether every caregiver can log the same baby, which languages the app supports, and what is free versus paid.

Where Baby Tracker - Newborn Log fits

It suits parents who want a long-established tracker with pumping logs and on-device timing predictions.

Where Deerly fits

Deerly suits families who want logging by voice and from widgets, and an Apple Watch nursing timer. Family logging with real-time sync is listed as free, and each entry shows who logged it.

Any of these apps can replace a paper log. Deerly focuses on getting entries in quickly: voice logging, widgets, an Apple Watch nursing timer, and family logging with real-time sync listed as free. It is in English and Traditional Chinese only, and the free plan shows ads.

FAQ

Can I log by voice in Deerly?

Yes. Say one sentence such as "120 ml and a dirty diaper" and it becomes separate records. The free plan has a usage quota.

Is family sharing free?

Yes. Logging the same baby with your partner or grandparents, with real-time sync, is part of the free plan.

Does Deerly work on Apple Watch?

Deerly has an Apple Watch nursing timer to start, switch sides and pause from the wrist. It is iOS only.
